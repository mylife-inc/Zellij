# Principles

A proof connects precise assumptions to a clear conclusion. A useful invariant
stays fixed while the surrounding state changes.

## An invariant

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, expanding the square gives

$$
(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2.
$$

For $n$ positive integers, the familiar sum is

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
$$

This is a **structural** argument, not just a numerical observation.[^proof]

[^proof]: A proof establishes every case allowed by its assumptions.
