> [!DEFINITION] {#def:positive} Positive numbers
>
> A real number $x$ is positive when $x>0$.

> [!LEMMA] {#lem:square} A square is nonnegative
>
> For real $x$, we have $x^2\geq 0$.

> [!THEOREM] {#thm:energy} Energy & equality
>
> By $\autoref{lem:square}$, the quantity is **nonnegative**. The assumptions are:
>
> - The mass is positive.
> - The speed is real.
>
> An ordinary quote inside the argument remains a quote:
>
> > Equality is a special case.
>
> ```text
> energy = mass * speed * speed
> ```
>
> The footnote gives a qualification.[^qualification]

> [!PROOF] {#proof:energy} Of the energy theorem
>
> Apply $\autoref{lem:square}$ and multiply by the positive mass. This proves
> $\autoref{thm:energy}$; compare $\autoref{prop:sum}$.

> [!COROLLARY] {#cor:zero} Zero speed
>
> At zero speed the energy is zero.
