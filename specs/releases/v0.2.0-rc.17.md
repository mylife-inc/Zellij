# Zellij CLI 0.2.0-rc.17

The downloaded rich renderer and matching engine images now preserve newer
keyboard-focus and pointer choices during desktop menu resizing. When links
move into or out of More, a delayed restoration no longer pulls focus back into
the menu after the reader has moved elsewhere. Automatic restoration avoids
scrolling and still works when the removed navigation control needs a visible
replacement.

The correction has a controlled Chromium/WebKit regression that fails on the
previous engine. The final full source browser suite passes 552 cases with
retries disabled. Two test-only races found during acceptance are also corrected;
their original failure traces and explanations remain in the implementation
record. Neither is presented as the cause of the two older unexplained browser
timing findings.

Rich build, check, watch and view continue to use the verified renderer under
`$ZELLIJ_HOME` (default `~/Dev/config/zellij`) without requiring user-installed
Docker, Node.js or Python. The small CLI downloads its matching renderer on
first use; `--offline` reuses an already downloaded version. Upgrading the CLI
does not rewrite authored documentation. Template identities are unchanged.

This candidate does not migrate HTTP static exports, Next.js development or
PDF typesetting to cached toolchains; their documented prerequisites remain.
Container-image commands still require a supported container runtime. WinGet
catalog review and deferred live provider verification are separate work.

This remains a pre-1.0 development candidate, not a claim of zero undiscovered
bugs. See the [focus investigation](../zellij-overflow-focus-2026-11-21.md) for
source acceptance and the [current readiness record](../zellij-readiness-2026-11-21.md)
for delivery status.
