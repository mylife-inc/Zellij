# Zellij CLI 0.2.0-rc.16

The product-launch, software-platform and creative-studio website starters now
include updated authoring guides at template version 0.1.3. Their instructions
explain that rich build, check and view use the downloaded, cached renderer and
do not require Docker, Node.js or Python. Generated rich sites open directly
from `site/index.html`; offline rebuilds reuse a renderer downloaded previously.

The template designs and runtime behavior are unchanged. Existing authored
sites are not rewritten by upgrading the CLI. Published 0.1.2 template archives
retain their original bytes; the revised guides have new template identities.
The other six templates retain their existing versions and archive identities.

WinGet manifests continue to accompany the release, but installation by the
public catalog ID remains pending Microsoft's review of the rc.15 submission.
Use the standalone installer, Homebrew or Scoop until catalog availability is
announced. Package managers retain ownership of their executable locations;
`ZELLIJ_HOME` retains its resource/cache role.

This remains a pre-1.0 development candidate. Hosted/container/PDF prerequisites
and the separately recorded deployment and browser-verification gaps are
unchanged.
