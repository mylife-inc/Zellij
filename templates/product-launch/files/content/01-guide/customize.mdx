# Artwork and navigation

The original local SVG illustrations are in `content/assets/`. Replace the
image path in the relevant section YAML with your own local asset. For compose
images, update `alt` too. Keep informative text in the section, not only inside
an illustration. No external fonts, tracking scripts or image services are
required. See `ASSETS.md` for provenance.

## Your company and product

The site name links to the website home. A separate company mark is optional:
add `brand.company` to `content/site.yaml`, using your own logo and URL.

```yaml
brand:
  company:
    src: assets/company.svg
    alt: Your company
    href: https://example.com
    newTab: true
```

Add that local image before enabling the example. `newTab: false` keeps the
company website in the same tab. The product name still returns home.

## Color, motion and links

`theme` selects the type and spacing system; `mode` accepts `light`, `dark`
or `system`. Theme colors adapt in both modes. Authored SVG artwork retains its
own palette. `motion: none` removes motion; reduced-motion preferences are
already respected. The page content remains readable without JavaScript.

Navigation comes from `menu.yaml`. Buttons link to working sample pages, so
replace their destinations when you add your own contact, purchase or signup
flow. There is no payment, form submission or account service behind this starter.
