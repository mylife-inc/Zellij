# Customize the design

The `kie` theme follows the typography, blue palette, pill buttons, wide headings
and card geometry of kie.ai. It is a Zellij theme, not an integration with that
service. The sample copy and illustrations are original and editable.

## Choose a mode

Set `mode: light`, `dark` or `system` in `content/site.yaml`. The theme button
lets readers choose. Space Grotesk is bundled locally; no font service is needed.

## Add your company

Add `brand.company` to `content/site.yaml` with `src`, `alt`, `href` and `newTab`.
The company link stays at the far left, the product/menu group is centered, and
search and theme controls stay at the right. Small screens use the shared drawer.

## Replace the illustrations

The three SVG files in `content/assets/` are original illustrations. Replace
those references in `content/sections/home/models.yaml` with your own images.
Keep meaningful descriptions next to them. See `ASSETS.md` for provenance.

## Keep the content useful

Use real prices, supported features and working calls to action. Every sample
button currently leads to another page in this starter. It includes no signup,
payment processing, remote tracking or customer endorsements.
