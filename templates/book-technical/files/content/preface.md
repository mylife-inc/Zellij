# Preface

A useful proof is an explanation another reader can inspect. This short
handbook begins with a familiar sum and develops a reusable way of reasoning.
Read with pencil and paper: examples suggest a claim, but the argument must
cover every case in its stated domain.

Each chapter pairs an idea with a worked example and a question. Numbered
equations and statements can be followed on the website or in the printed book.
The [glossary](glossary:invariant) introduces the central vocabulary.
