# The open window

## A passage

> The room had not changed, yet opening the window altered everything.
> A distant conversation, the movement of a branch, the cool edge of morning:
> what seemed empty had been waiting for attention.

## Commentary

The passage turns on a small action. Nothing is acquired, and nothing is solved.
The reader is invited to notice what was already present. Here,
[attention](glossary:attention) means a deliberate return to the particular,
rather than an attempt to notice everything at once.[^attention]
