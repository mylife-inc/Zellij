# Notes for a reading group

Begin by giving everyone time to read in silence. Invite each reader to share
one detail before asking for an interpretation. Distinguish the words of the
passage from the experiences and assumptions that each person brings.

Return to $\autoref{example:journal}$ for a simple writing exercise. The
[attention](index:attention) and [reflection](index:reflection) entries provide
starting points for a discussion across chapters.

## Keeping the conversation open

- Ask what led a reader to their interpretation.
- Allow a question to remain unanswered.
- End with a passage someone would like to revisit.
