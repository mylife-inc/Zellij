# Preface

This is a place for reading slowly. Each chapter begins with an original short
passage, follows it with commentary and ends with questions to carry into daily
life. The passages are sample writing; replace them with your own material or
properly attributed sources you have permission to use.

Read a passage once for its sound, once for its meaning and once for the
question it leaves behind. Keep a notebook beside the book. The
[glossary](glossary:attention) gathers the vocabulary used throughout.
